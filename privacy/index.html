Privacy

Effective date: 14 November 2026.

TapDose is made by an independent developer. This policy covers the TapDose app and this website.

TapDose collects no data. There are no accounts and no analytics.

Your medications and dose log stay on your phone. The app makes no network calls except opening these web pages. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include TapDose data, as it does for other apps.

NFC stickers hold only a random ID inside a link. They contain no medication information.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept on your phone until you delete it. Deleting the app deletes all TapDose data on the phone; copies in your phone's backup follow your backup settings.

If your phone has no backup and is lost or reset, your TapDose history is lost.
